Blockchain / Web3 Explainer

Polygon Surges With a New Web3 ID Service

Polygon introduced a zero-knowledge proof-based identity solution designed to enable self-sovereign, private verification without exposing unnecessary user data.

Polygon has again made the news for a very good reason. The company has just introduced a zero-knowledge proof-based identity solution: a new decentralized identification service. Developers can use the service built on zero-knowledge proofs to build self-sovereign, decentralized, and private identification solutions. The Polygon ID solution uses zero-knowledge proofs to ensure that only the absolutely necessary information is communicated between participants in a transaction, while the rest is kept private. Major companies have already adopted Polygon ID. The peer-to-peer payment system DePay, the digital passport app Clique, and the token-gating platform Guild.xyz are among the initiatives already integrated. The Sandbox, a leading game platform and metaverse ecosystem, has also integrated Polygon ID.
